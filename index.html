How to Calculate Days Between Two Dates

Enter a starting date and an ending date, then press Calculate. The calculator will determine the number of days between the two dates.

If you select business days, Saturdays and Sundays will be excluded from the calculation.

What Can This Calculator Calculate?

This tool can calculate the total number of calendar days, weeks, approximate months, approximate years and business days between two dates.
